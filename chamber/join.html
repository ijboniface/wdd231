NP Membership

Cost: Free

  • Listing in the chamber member directory.
  • Invitations to community networking events.
  • Monthly chamber newsletter.
  • Access to non-profit partnership opportunities.

Bronze Membership

Cost: ₦25,000 per year

  • Everything in the NP level.
  • Standard directory listing with logo.
  • Member discounts on chamber events.
  • Access to quarterly business training.

Silver Membership

Cost: ₦50,000 per year

  • Everything in the Bronze level.
  • Featured directory listing.
  • Larger discounts on events and training.
  • Chance to appear in the home page member spotlight.

Gold Membership

Cost: ₦100,000 per year

  • Everything in the Silver level.
  • Priority home page spotlight position.
  • Free admission to chamber events.
  • Advertising space on the chamber website and at events.
