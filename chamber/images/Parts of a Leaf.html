Parts of a Leaf

A leaf is an essential part of a plant that plays a crucial role in photosynthesis. The main parts of a leaf include:

  • Blade: The broad, flat part of the leaf that captures sunlight.
  • Petiole: The stalk that connects the leaf blade to the stem.
  • Veins: The vascular tissue that transports water, nutrients, and food throughout the leaf.
  • Margin: The edge of the leaf, which can be smooth, serrated, or lobed.
  • Apex: The tip of the leaf.
  • Base: The part of the leaf that attaches to the petiole.
