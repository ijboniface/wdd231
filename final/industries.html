Industries & regions

Where the stories are being made.

Christian cinema in Africa is not one industry. It is a collection of production ministries, independent studios, festivals, training communities and filmmakers working through different cultural contexts.

Nigeria · West Africa

Heritage + scale

Mount Zion Film Productions maintains a substantial online catalogue spanning classic titles, contemporary films and series. Its current catalogue includes both English- and Yoruba-language works and categories such as Church & Ministry, Family & Parenting, Marriage, Rapture and Youth & Career.

Directory lens: heritage, spiritual conflict, family, ministry and contemporary faith stories.

Sources: Mount Zion's official movies catalogue and Agbáraǹlá: The Return official site.

Kenya · East Africa

Faith + culture

Where the River Divides is a Kenyan Christian short film inspired by a true story and set around a young man's decision between Christian faith and his father's traditional legacy. In 2026 it won Best Film and six other awards at the first Africa International Christian Film Festival.

Directory lens: faith, sacrifice, calling, history and cultural identity.

Sources: NTA's 2026 festival report and the Kenya Film Commission's industry overview.

Ghana · West Africa

Faith + universal stories

KAC Studios has publicly called for collaboration to improve Christian film production in Africa and for stories that can connect with audiences beyond church communities. Its reported catalogue includes The Cleaner, Mirage and The Right to Do Anything, exploring themes such as family reconciliation, mental health, love, isolation and career struggles.

Directory lens: accessible storytelling, production quality and universal human themes.

Source: Ghana News Agency, February 2026.

Uganda · East Africa

Training + mission

John 316 Film School Foundation describes itself as a Ugandan Christian film school focused on practical training, mentorship and production. Its project archive shows recurring student shorts as well as newer feature and short productions, giving emerging filmmakers hands-on experience.

Directory lens: skills development, mentorship, student production and emerging filmmakers.

Sources: John 316 Film School Foundation and its current projects archive.

South Africa · Southern Africa

Networks + professional practice

Christian Filmmakers Network presents itself as a South African faith-based film organisation supporting filmmakers through training, production resources and distribution opportunities. Its stated mission combines faith-driven storytelling with a sustainable professional approach.

Directory lens: collaboration, training, production support and distribution.

Source: Christian Filmmakers Network, including its mission and services pages.

Continental ecosystem · 2026

Connection + industry building

Africa International Christian Film Festival and ICVM's MediaSphere Africa illustrate a growing continental conversation around Christian film: screenings, masterclasses, networking, distribution, investment and skills development.

Directory lens: festivals, mentorship, collaboration, distribution and sustainable industry growth.

Sources: NTA's AICFF report and ICVM's MediaSphere Africa 2026 overview.

The bigger picture

From ministry media to cinematic language.

African Christian cinema is increasingly negotiating the same questions facing the wider film industry: how to tell culturally specific stories, how to reach audiences outside existing communities, how to finance quality productions, and how to distribute them beyond traditional channels.

Gospelwood's editorial rule
