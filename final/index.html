What is Gospelwood?

A directory for a growing storytelling movement.

Gospelwood is the Christian-cinema layer of the African Film Directory: a curated, student-built record of films, filmmakers, production communities and stories shaped by faith.

It is not a streaming service or a church bulletin. It is a starting point for discovering what African Christian filmmakers have made, where they are making it, and how the stories are changing.

Heritage → Excellence → Future

Heritage: Nigeria's long-running gospel-film tradition.

Excellence: African faith films reaching festivals and wider audiences.

Future: training communities developing the next generation of filmmakers.

A moment to watch

Agbara Nla returns to the big screen.

1993 → 2026

From VHS-era classic to cinema event.

The original Agbara Nla was released in 1993. Its English presentation followed as The Ultimate Power in 1994. In 2026, Agbara Nla: The Return brought the story back as a contemporary cinema production.

Reported preview result: about ₦70 million from 12,134 admissions between September 25 and October 1, according to Nigerian Box Office reporting cited by contemporary coverage. The distributor reported more than ₦71 million. These figures are reported industry figures, not an independently audited result.

Across the continent

More than one story of Christian cinema.

Different countries are building different pathways: established production ministries, festival recognition, studio experimentation and film training.

Nigeria · West Africa

Heritage + scale

Mount Zion's archive shows decades of Christian film production, from early classics to contemporary features and series.

Kenya · East Africa

Faith + culture

Where the River Divides connected Christian faith with Kenyan history and received major recognition at the 2026 Africa International Christian Film Festival.

Ghana · West Africa

Faith + universal stories

KAC Studios is explicitly pushing for Christian-inspired stories that can connect beyond church audiences.

Uganda · East Africa

Training + mission

John 316 Film School Foundation combines filmmaking education with Christian purpose, producing student and feature projects.
